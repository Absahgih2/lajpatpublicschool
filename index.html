Admission Journey

Joining LPS in five simple steps

Admissions ordinarily open in January for the session beginning in April.

  1. Step 01

    Enquire & Collect the Form

    Visit the school office or call +91 90313 88358 to collect the registration form and understand the current fee structure.

  2. Step 02

    Submit Documents

    Birth Certificate (mandatory for LKG), Transfer Certificate from the previous school, last report card, photographs and address proof.

  3. Step 03

    Entrance Test

    For admission to classes above LKG an entrance test is conducted. The prescribed age criteria for the class is strictly adhered to.

  4. Step 04

    Interaction & Confirmation

    A short interaction with the parents and child. The Principal reserves the right to accept or refuse any application.

  5. Step 05

    Welcome to the Family

    Complete the admission formalities, collect the uniform, diary and ID card — and begin a wonderful journey at LPS.

Questions Parents Ask

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Tap Laju, our school buddy in the corner — he answers instantly.

Which is a good school in Jamshedpur for value-based education?
Lajpat Public School in Golmuri, Jamshedpur offers value-based holistic education combining an English-medium academic curriculum with moral education, sports, dance, music, dramatics and adventure activities — all within a caring, disciplined campus near Howrah Bridge.
When do admissions open at Lajpat Public School?
Admissions ordinarily open in January for the session beginning in April. For LKG a birth certificate is required; for other classes an entrance test is conducted and a Transfer Certificate from the previous school is mandatory.
What are the school and office timings?
School runs Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM. The office and fee counter operate 8:00 AM – 12:00 noon on working days. The Principal can be met between 8–9 AM on Monday, Wednesday and Saturday with a prior appointment.
What is the minimum attendance required for examinations?
A minimum of 75% attendance is compulsory for a student to be allowed to sit for the final examination. Absence beyond two days without prior information and sanction may lead to the student's name being struck off the rolls.
How is promotion to the next class decided?
Promotion is based on performance throughout the year — weekly tests, term examinations and the final exam. A student needs the minimum required marks in all scholastic subjects and at least grade D in non-scholastic subjects. Regularity in homework is also considered, and supplementary examinations are not conducted.
What are the fee payment rules?
Fees are accepted in the school office from the 1st to the 20th of every month, 8:00 AM – 12:00 noon. From the 21st a late fine of ₹20 applies; previous month dues paid between the 1st–15th carry a ₹50 fine. A Transfer Certificate is issued for ₹200 once all dues are cleared.
Are scholarships or fee concessions available?
Yes. Seats are reserved for talented students from the weaker sections of society whose parents cannot afford to pay the fees and other dues. Please speak to the school office for details.
Where exactly is the school located?
New Kalimati Road, near Howrah Bridge, Tuiladungri, Golmuri, Jamshedpur, Jharkhand 831007. Call +91 90313 88358 for directions.
