Examinations & Promotion

Progress measured honestly

Promotion is decided on the basis of performance throughout the year — not a single day's paper.

  • Based on weekly tests, term examinations and the final examination
  • Regularity in doing homework is also taken into consideration
  • Minimum required marks in all scholastic subjects are necessary
  • At least grade D in all non-scholastic subjects
  • Use of unfair means may lead to expulsion from the school
  • Supplementary examinations are not conducted

Attendance Policy

Being present is half the learning

75% Minimum

A minimum of 75% attendance is compulsory for a student to be allowed to sit for the final examination.

Absence Beyond Two Days

Absence for more than two days without prior information and sanction may lead to the student's name being struck off the rolls.

Medical Certificate

Required for absence over a long period, and always for a contagious or infectious illness.

Late Coming

Late arrivals are recorded by circling the date in the school calendar. Repeated lateness invites disciplinary action.

Re-entering Class

After absence or lateness, the student must show the diary signed by the parent to the teacher in charge or the co-ordinator.

Leave

No leave is sanctioned during the school year except on holidays stated in the school calendar, or in genuine unforeseen circumstances.
