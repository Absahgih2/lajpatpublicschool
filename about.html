Our Story

A school built on enquiry and empathy

Named in the spirit of Lala Lajpat Rai, our school stands in Golmuri, Jamshedpur with a simple conviction: that real education must shape both the intellect and the heart.

We believe a child who can solve an equation but cannot show kindness has only been half taught. That is why every academic year at LPS carries an equal weight of moral learning, creative expression and physical growth.

  • English medium instruction with Hindi and Sanskrit given real importance
  • Moral education imparted for genuine character formation
  • Continuous, objective evaluation of academic work and conduct
  • A caring campus where corporal punishment has no place

Moral Education

Modernity with eternal values

Moral education is imparted for character formation. Our aim is to mould the student into a model of modernity in which the spiritual aspect has its rightful importance.

  • Every Saturday students learn Vedic mantras, devotional songs and quotations
  • Hawan is performed on Saturdays with teachers and students together
  • Dharma Shiksha books guide students in moral values
  • Stress is laid on the eternal values of Bhartiya Sanskriti
  • Discussion of important current events builds awareness and judgement

School Rules & Discipline

Order that grows from respect

Discipline at LPS is built through guidance and counselling — not fear.

Conduct & Respect

Students are expected to address teachers and all staff with respect and politeness, and to behave with honour on the way to and from school.

Serious Misconduct

Habitual disobedience, immorality of any kind or serious disrespect towards authority constitute sufficient reason for disciplinary action, including dismissal.

No Corporal Punishment

Teachers correct and guide through counselling and a system of objective evaluation. A record of work and conduct is carefully maintained for each student.

Uniform & Punctuality

Much emphasis is placed on correct wearing of the uniform, regular attendance, speaking correct English and healthy habits.

Identity Card

Students must carry their ID card daily. If it is lost or damaged, the cost of issuing a new card will be realised from the student.

Prohibited Items

No books, magazines or articles of an objectionable nature may be brought onto the school premises. Students are responsible for their own belongings.
