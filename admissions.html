Admission Process

Five steps to a new beginning

  1. Step 01

    Enquire & Collect the Form

    Visit the school office or call +91 90313 88358 to collect the registration form and understand the current fee structure.

  2. Step 02

    Submit the Documents

    Birth Certificate (mandatory for LKG), Transfer Certificate from the last school, previous report card, photographs and address proof.

  3. Step 03

    Entrance Test

    For admission to any class above LKG, an entrance test is conducted. The prescribed age criteria for the class will be adhered to.

  4. Step 04

    Interaction & Decision

    A short interaction with parents and child. The Principal reserves the right to accept or refuse any application without assigning a reason.

  5. Step 05

    Complete the Formalities

    Pay the admission dues, collect the uniform, diary and identity card — and welcome to the LPS family.

Documents Required

What to bring with you

Keeping these ready makes the whole process smooth and quick for your family.

  • Birth Certificate — mandatory for admission to LKG
  • Transfer Certificate from the school last attended
  • Previous class report card / marksheet
  • Recent passport-size photographs of the student
  • Aadhaar copies of the student and both parents
  • Address proof of the family residence
LKG Onwards Entrance Test Age Criteria Applies Limited Seats

Seats for Deserving Children

Seats are reserved for talented students from the weaker sections of society whose parents cannot afford to pay the fees and other dues. If this applies to your family, please speak to the school office — we will guide you personally and confidentially.

Withdrawal Policy

If you ever need to move on

One Month's Notice

A clear one month's notice in writing, or one month's fee in lieu thereof, must be given for the withdrawal of a pupil.

Rejoining Later

If a student is withdrawn and the parents later wish for re-admission, an entrance test will be held and the registration fee as applicable will be charged.

Transfer Certificate

A written application by the parent or guardian is required. The certificate is issued for ₹200 only after all school dues are paid.
